## 📚 How to Use TCV-ModMaster

### Installing Your First Mod

1. Open TCV-ModMaster after installation.
2. Click on “Add Mod” or the plus icon.
3. Paste a link from GameBanana or Nexus Mods. Alternatively, drag and drop a mod file.
4. Click “Install.” The mod will be downloaded and placed in the correct folder.
5. Enable the mod from the list. You can also choose a profile for it.

### Switching Between Mods

- In the main window, you will see a list of installed mods.
- Use the checkboxes next to each mod to enable or disable them.
- To switch profiles, click the profile name at the top and select a different one. All mods assigned to that profile will load automatically.

### Updating a Mod

- TCV-ModMaster will show a notification when updates are available.
- Click “Update All” to update everything, or update individual mods from the list.
